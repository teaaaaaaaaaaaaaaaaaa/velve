==================================================
CATEGORY-SPECIFIC DISPLAY SYSTEM (IMPORTANT)
==================================================

The browsing experience should change depending on the product category.

Do NOT display every item the same way.

Each clothing category should feel like it belongs in a real boutique.

Examples:

T-Shirts
- Hang from standard wooden hangers.
- Slightly oversized.
- Soft fabric movement.

Hoodies
- Hang from thicker premium hangers.
- Slightly heavier physics.
- More swing due to weight.

Jackets
- Premium wooden hangers.
- Stronger shoulder shape.
- Slightly lower hanging position.
- Heavier spring animation.

Shirts
- Thin luxury wooden hangers.
- Smaller swing.
- Cleaner presentation.

Pants
- Hang using clip hangers.
- Legs naturally fall downward.
- Slight fabric movement.

Skirts
- Clip hangers.
- Fabric naturally drapes.

Dresses
- Longer hangers.
- More elegant swing.

Bags
- Hang from metal hooks attached to the rack.
- Swing independently.

Shoes
- Do NOT hang.
- Display them on a premium floating shelf positioned directly below the clothing rack.
- The shelf scrolls together with the rack.
- Shoes receive soft contact shadows.
- The selected pair rises slightly from the shelf.

Accessories
- Small floating display hooks.
- Minimal elegant presentation.

The transition between categories should feel seamless.

The rack system should adapt automatically based on the category.

Users should instantly feel like they entered a different section of a luxury boutique.

This interaction should become one of Velve's unique brand signatures.
